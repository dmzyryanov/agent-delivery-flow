# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- The delivery flow ported from QA Tools to GitHub: the roles, the delivery
  cycle and the scheduled grooming, the delivery queue and lock, the Bash
  guard, the review artifact and fingerprints, outside review (off by
  default), versioning and recovery.
- `/flow-init` sets a project up from this template; `/decompose` turns a big
  goal into an epic and small issues.
